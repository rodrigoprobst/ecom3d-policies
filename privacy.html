Privacy Policy

ecom3d · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-08 · Controller contact and Data Protection Officer (Encarregado): Rodrigo B. Probst · rodbeka@gmail.com

Who we are

ecom3d is a self-hosted application, developed and operated by an individual developer in Brazil, that keeps a seller's product catalog, stock and prices in sync with marketplaces such as TikTok Shop and Mercado Livre. When a seller connects a marketplace account, ecom3d processes data on behalf of that seller, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018).

Data we process

  • Seller data: shop name and identifiers, warehouse identifier, and the access tokens granted by the seller.
  • Product data: listings, prices, stock and categories.
  • Order data: products, quantities, amounts and status. From the buyer we keep only the nickname, name, city and state of the shipment. We do not store street address, tax ID (CPF), phone number or e-mail.

Why we process it

Only to fulfil and manage the seller's orders: update stock when a sale happens, keep prices and stock in sync, and handle cancellations and returns. Data is never sold, never shared with third parties and never used for marketing or profiling.

Where it is stored

In Brazil only, on a single encrypted workstation. Data travels encrypted (TLS) between the marketplaces and the workstation and is not stored by any intermediary.

How long we keep it

  • Buyer nickname and name are erased 90 days after the order is delivered or cancelled. Sales records are kept without personal data.
  • When a seller disconnects the marketplace account or the relationship ends, access tokens are deleted immediately and all data obtained from that marketplace is deleted within 30 days.

How we protect it

Encryption at rest and in transit, least-privilege access, multi-factor authentication and continuous vulnerability management, as described in our Security Policy.

Your rights

Under the LGPD you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization or deletion of your personal data, and information about sharing. Send your request to rodbeka@gmail.com; we answer within 15 days. Requests received through a seller or TikTok Shop are handled within the same period. You may also file a complaint with the Brazilian data protection authority (ANPD).

Data breaches

If an incident affects personal data, we notify TikTok Shop and the affected sellers within 24 hours of confirmation, and the ANPD and affected individuals when required by law.

Changes

This policy is reviewed at least once a year. The version and effective date at the top show the latest update.
